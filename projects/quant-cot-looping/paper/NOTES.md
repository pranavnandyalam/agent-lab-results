# Paper notes: known weaknesses (for the Lead / reviewers)

The evidence supports a short workshop-style negative result, not a full conference paper. Weaknesses a reviewer will raise:

1. Scale: one model (Qwen3-0.6B), 16 GSM8K problems, 3 sampling seeds (effective n = 16). CIs are wide (w5g64 share 0.00-0.51).
2. Censoring: 2048-token cap; fp32 itself truncates 17/48, so A_q is a lower bound and shares could move either way with a larger budget.
3. Detector: hand-set thresholds from 8 train problems; recall unmeasured; precision audit is single-rater, excerpt-only, and only 6 flagged traces come from non-collapsed levels. H1 is not robust to the 12-gram variant (w4g64 CI up to 0.76). Possible problem-text leak in rule A (audit items 3, 6), effect unquantified.
4. Quantization realism: simulated RTN only (no GPTQ/AWQ/GGUF); w3g32 confounds bits with group size; collapse level may differ for real formats.
5. H1 is refuted only at 4.5-5.5 effective bits; the collapsed level (where looping dominates) is excluded by design.
6. H2 at w4g64 passes only by median rule with exactly 15 pairs and a CI crossing +30%; selection bias toward short traces.
7. H3 is near-tautological (scoring rule ties truncation to incorrectness); no claim made.
8. Test problems were reused from the sanity trial that picked the levels.
9. Accuracy uses a truncation-penalizing scoring rule (naive vs scored correct: fp32 30/27, w5g64 26/24, w4g64 16/15).
10. Uncertainty is given as bootstrap 95% CIs over problems plus per-seed accuracy, not mean +- std; no std numbers were added because they are not in RESULTS.md.
11. Related-work details for 2606.02011 (loop rule), 2606.25519 (models, embedding-based repetition) and 2609.26708 (Qwen3-0.6B budget exhaustion / repeated 8-grams) were checked on the arXiv HTML full text via a summarising fetch tool, not read line by line.

Figures are regenerated by `paper/make_figures.py` (needs numpy + matplotlib; the project .venv has no matplotlib, a temporary venv was used). The script asserts per-level means/truncation/accuracy equal results/core/analysis.json.
