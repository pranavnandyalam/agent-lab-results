Verdict: refuted under the pre-registered 20-gram detector (and 16-gram), at 4.5-5.5 effective bits only (the level where looping is obvious, w3g32, is excluded by design), within the 2048 cap; not robust to looser definitions (12-gram CI reaches 0.76) and detector recall is unmeasured. At 12-gram the point estimate is below 0.5 but the CI is wide (inconclusive). Share is not monotone under the primary detector (only under 12-gram). Most added length at 4.5-5.5 bits is therefore not detected as 20-gram loops; it may include soft repetition the detector misses (the audit found soft loops, and there is no recall estimate). A 20-token exact repeat is a conservative loop definition; looser "soft" repetition (see audit) is not counted.

## H3 (exploratory): what predicts a wrong answer?
AUROC for incorrectness over all traces (primary detector): total length 0.92 (fp32), 1.00 (w5g64), 1.00 (w4g64); truncation 0.86/1.00/0.97; loop fraction 0.51 (p=.75)/0.67 (p=.003)/0.63 (p=.055). Length and truncation nearly coincide with incorrectness through the scoring rule (fp32: 1 truncated trace scored correct; 2 finished-incorrect at w4g64), so they should not be read as findings. Among non-truncated traces there are too few wrong ones (fp32 5, w5g64 0, w4g64 2) for any claim. Loop fraction with the 16/12-gram detector reaches AUROC 0.78-0.85 at w4g64 (all traces), but again mostly reflects truncation correlation. No confirm/refute claim. For completeness, 12-gram loop-fraction AUROC on non-truncated traces: fp32 0.71 (p=.048, 5 incorrect), w4g64 0.97 (p=.014, 2 incorrect).

## Precision audit (20 flagged traces, one rater, excerpts only; `results/core/audit_labels.md`)
12 degenerate loops, 4 soft, 4 false positives (precision 0.80, strict 0.60). All 12 degenerate cases are w3g32 traces (12/14 of that level). At fp32/w5g64/w4g64 only 6 flagged traces were sampled: 0 degenerate, 3 soft, 3 false positives. So at the non-collapsed levels the detector over-counts degenerate looping (the precision audit points toward over-counting, recall is unmeasured, and this rests on only 6 flagged traces from one rater reading excerpts, and says nothing about missed loops). No recall estimate exists beyond dev (DEV.md: some truncated traces loop on short phrases below 20 tokens and are missed).

## Qualitative finding
Qwen3-0.6B at 3 weight bits (g32, 4.0 effective) collapses completely: every trace hits the cap, 46/48 flagged as heavily looping (mean 648 loop tokens, ~32% of tokens), with stuck phrases like "Let me focus on the problem". At 4.5 and 5.5 effective bits the added length is mostly not detected as exact-repeat loops (soft repetition is not ruled out); more traces hit the cap.

## Limitations
One 0.6B model; simulated RTN not a deployed format; 16 problems (the same 16 were used in the earlier throughput/KL sanity trial that chose the levels); 2048 cap censors lengths; single-rater audit from excerpts; hand-chosen detector thresholds (frozen on 8 train problems); seeds are sampling seeds on the same problems, so effective n is 16; three bit levels only, with a group-size confound at the lowest.

Possible detector leak: audit items 3 and 6 are flagged spans re-quoting the problem text, which rule A should ignore (likely token-boundary mismatch); its effect on the shares is unquantified.
